# UVAcars

The official desktop ACARS client for [UVA virtual airline](https://www.flyuva.org/).

UVAcars is a flight tracker built for UVA's pilots. It connects to Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020 / 2024) and X-Plane 11 / 12, logs your flights in real time, and files them automatically to the UVA crew center when you land.

## Download

Grab the latest installer from the [Releases page](https://github.com/NorthAxisDigital/UVAcars-releases/releases/latest).

- **Windows**: `UVAcars-Setup-x.y.z.exe`
- **macOS**: `UVAcars-Setup-x.y.z.dmg` (universal — Apple Silicon and Intel)

Once installed, UVAcars updates itself automatically when new releases ship — you'll see a banner inside the app whenever an update is ready.

## Who's this for?

Only active UVA pilots — you'll need a UVA crew center account to sign in. Not a UVA pilot yet? Apply at [flyuva.org](https://www.flyuva.org/).

## Reporting a problem

The easiest way is the built-in **Report an Issue** button inside UVAcars — it automatically attaches your app version, simulator, and recent log, so you don't have to explain the technical bits. You can also post in the `#uvacars-issues-feedback` channel on the UVA Discord. Helpful details:

- What you were doing when the problem hit
- A screenshot if there's anything visual
- Your sim (MSFS / X-Plane), your aircraft, and the route
